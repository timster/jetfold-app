Try on a theme

Each theme colors the whole window, from the sidebar to the editor, and comes in light and dark.

Light, dark or automatic

Follow your Mac's appearance, or keep a theme light or dark all the time. Pick both in Settings → General.

Everything matches

Selections, palettes, popups and git colors all take the theme's colors, so nothing looks borrowed.

A theme of your own

Give a notes folder its own colors with a .jetfold/theme.json that changes any theme. It travels with the folder.
