What's new in Jetfold 2

Room to think in space, and more of your Mac in your notes. Everything here is a free update, and like the rest of Jetfold, every part of it can stay out of your way.

Canvases

A board for thinking in space. Lay out text, your notes, images, and links, join them with arrows, and frame them in groups.

  • Edit notes right on their cards
  • Text cards in Markdown, with tasks, links, and math
  • Arrows with labels, colors, and groups
  • Export as PDF, PNG, a web page, or Markdown
  • Saved as open JSON Canvas files
Canvases in the guide

Straight from your iPhone

Right-click in a note or on a canvas and use your iPhone or iPad: take a photo, scan a few pages, or draw a sketch. It lands where you clicked, saved with your other images.

Your iPhone in the guide

Every word in your pictures

Jetfold reads the text in your photos and scanned PDFs, on your Mac, so search finds the note with the whiteboard on it.

  • Search in Notes matches words in images and PDFs
  • Copy Text from Image puts them on the clipboard
  • Never leaves your Mac
Text in images in the guide

Today, on your desktop

The Today widget shows what's due in your notes: overdue tasks, today, tomorrow, and what's coming up. Click one to open the note at that line.

  • Small, medium, and large sizes
  • On the desktop or in Notification Center
The widget in the guide

Focus filters

Keep work notes and your own apart. A Focus can open the notes folder you choose, and a view, tag, or folder in it, then go back when it ends.

Focus filters in the guide

A toolbar of your own

Choose View → Customize Toolbar… and drag in the buttons you reach for, like Back and Forward, Today's Note, or New Canvas.

The toolbar in the guide

And also

Smaller things that make a difference.

  • Deleted notes can go to a .trash folder in your notes folder, where you and your sync see them, instead of the system Trash.
  • The inbox can be the notes folder itself: leave the folder setting empty.
  • jetfold://open links can put the cursor anywhere in a note.
  • Moving a folded heading up or down takes the folded section with it.
