Privacy

Jetfold doesn't collect any data about you or your notes.

The app

  • Your notes remain where you put them. Jetfold reads and writes the files in the folder you choose. There's no account or server, and no copy is kept anywhere else.
  • No analytics or tracking. Jetfold doesn't record how you use it and doesn't include any third-party code that does.
  • Settings are stored locally. Settings for a notes folder are kept in a .jetfold folder inside it. The list of folders you've opened is kept on your Mac.
  • Git works only on your Mac. Jetfold commits to the repository in your notes folder, but never pushes or pulls.
  • Text in pictures is recognized on your Mac. To make images and PDFs searchable, Jetfold uses the text recognition built into macOS. The recognized text is cached on your Mac, outside your notes folder, and never sent anywhere.
  • The Today widget receives only what's due. Jetfold passes your dated tasks and events to the widget through a folder on your Mac that only Jetfold and the widget can read.

When Jetfold uses the internet

Jetfold only connects to the internet for something you've asked for:

  • Link titles. When you paste a web address, Jetfold fetches that page to fill in the title. Turn this off in Settings → Editor → Fill in link titles when pasting a URL.
  • Web images. If a note shows an image from a web address, Jetfold loads it from there, as a browser would.

These requests go directly to the sites involved, and nothing is sent to the developer.

Purchases

The free trial and the unlock are App Store purchases, handled by Apple. Jetfold only checks whether you've bought them, and never sees your payment details.

This website

jetfold.app uses no cookies. It counts page visits with GoatCounter, which collects no personal data and doesn't track you across sites. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log visits as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. The theme you pick on the themes page is saved in your browser.
