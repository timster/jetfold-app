Everything is a keystroke away

Jump to any note, search every word you've written, or run any command without touching the mouse.

Connect your ideas

Link your notes and watch your thinking grow into something bigger. Rename or reorganize, and nothing breaks.

Plan your days

Dates in your notes come alive. Tasks and events from every note gather in one agenda, so nothing slips.

Git, without the terminal

See what changed, step back through any note's history, and bring back any version. Commit when you're ready.

Simple when you want it
