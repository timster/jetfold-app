Notes in **plain Markdown**, with the markup *out of sight*.

Jetfold is a native Mac app for a folder of plain .md files. Your notes remain on your Mac. You don't need an account, and nothing is tracked.

Beyond the page

More room to think

Lay out ideas that don't fit on a page, and add photos and sketches from your iPhone.

See all the features

  • Canvases

    Arrange notes, text cards and images on an endless board.

  • From your iPhone

    Add photos, scans, or sketches directly from your iPhone.

  • Text in pictures

    Search for words in your photos and scans.

  • Today widget

    The Today widget can be added to your Mac's desktop.

  • Focus filters

    Works with Focus filters built into your Mac.

  • Your toolbar

    Fully customizable toolbar, like a native Mac app should have.

Everything is a keystroke away

Open notes, search, and run commands without reaching for the mouse.

Connect your ideas

Type [[ to link to another note. Links update when you rename or move a note.

Plan your days

Tasks with dates and other dates in your notes appear in the Agenda.

Git, without the terminal

If your notes folder is a git repository, you can see what changed and restore earlier versions of a note. You choose when to commit.

Simple when you want it

If you only want a folder of notes and an editor, hide the rest in the View menu.

Try them on
Your notes are just files.

Open them in any editor, sync them with any service, and keep them in git.
Jetfold doesn't add metadata or change your formatting.

Get Jetfold for your Mac

Jetfold requires macOS 14 or later.
You don't need an account, and your notes don't leave your Mac unless you export or share them.
