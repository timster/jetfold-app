Where are my notes stored?
In the folder you choose, as ordinary .md and .txt files. Jetfold doesn't keep a database or a copy anywhere else.
Can I use the notes I already have?
Yes. Choose the folder they're in, and they appear right away, unchanged.
Does Jetfold sync?
Not on its own. Your notes are files in a folder, so you can sync them with iCloud Drive, Dropbox, git, or any other folder sync. Synced changes appear while Jetfold is open.
What happens if a note changes in two places?
If a note changes on disk while you have unsaved edits, a bar appears above the note with Keep Mine and Use Disk Version buttons. Nothing is overwritten until you choose.
Do I need git?
No. If your notes folder is a git repository, Jetfold shows changes and history and can commit for you. If it isn't, you won't see any git features.
Can I hide the features I don't use?
Yes. Turn off Tasks, Agenda, tags, the calendar, and your views in the View menu, and you see only your folders, notes, and the editor.

Any Mac with macOS 14 Sonoma or later.
How do I report a bug?
Choose Help → Send Feedback… in Jetfold to start an email to us with your Jetfold and macOS versions filled in. You can also write to {{email}} directly. Either way, tell us what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
