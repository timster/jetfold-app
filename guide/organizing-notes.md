# Folders and Notes

Jetfold's folders are the real folders on your disk, and notes are the real files. Anything you do in Jetfold happens to those files, and anything you do in Finder shows up in Jetfold right away.

## Folders

The sidebar shows your notes folders. Click a folder to show the notes in it. Arrows will collapse or expand folders.

![The sidebar: folders with note counts, a collapsed folder with a dimmed total, dots on folders with uncommitted changes, and the Tags group below](screenshots/05-sidebar.png)

Right-click a folder for folder options like creating a new note or subfolder. You can drag a folder onto another folder to move it.

Turn note counts on or off with **View → Note Counts**.

> Renaming or moving a folder updates every [wikilink](links.md) that points into it, so your links keep working.

## The note list

The middle column lists the notes in the selected folder or tag. With previews on, a line or two of the note text is shown.

![The note list header (folder name, sort menu, New Note button) and rows with bold titles, dates on the right, two-line previews, a pinned note, and git status letters](screenshots/07-note-list.png)

Use the **sort** button above the list to sort by title, date edited, or date created. **View → Note List** shows or hides previews, dates, and [git status](git.md#status-badges).

### Editing several notes at once

Select more than one note, and the editor shows them all, one after another. This is handy for reviewing a week of daily notes, or comparing two drafts.

![Three daily notes selected in the list and shown one after another in the editor](screenshots/08-stacked.png)

## Working with notes

Right-click a note, or use the **Note** menu or the **⋯** button at the top of the editor, for note options like renaming, pinning, and duplicating. With several notes selected, they apply to all of them.

- **Rename** a note by clicking the title at the top of the note. The file is renamed to match, and links to it are updated.
- **Pin to Top** keeps a note at the top of the list.
- **Make Read-Only** protects a note you don't want to change by accident.
- **Move** notes by dragging them onto a folder in the sidebar, or with **Note → Move to Folder…** (⇧⌘M).

Pins and read-only notes are kept in the workspace's settings, so your files don't change.

## Deleting notes

Deleted notes go to the macOS Trash.

## Note windows

Double-click a note to open it in a separate window, handy for keeping a reference note or a to-do list beside your other work. **Window → Keep on Top** keeps it in front of other apps.
