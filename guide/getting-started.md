# Getting Started

## Choosing your notes folder

The first time you open Jetfold, it asks for a folder. Choose any folder: an existing collection of Markdown files, a folder synced with iCloud Drive, a git repository, or a brand-new empty one. Jetfold leaves your files exactly as they are.

![The welcome screen in a small window: the origami jet floating above "Choose the folder that holds your notes." and the Open Folder… button](screenshots/02-welcome.png)

> **Tip:** Click the jet.

Jetfold treats a notes folder as a **workspace** and stores its settings in the folder. To open another workspace, choose **File → Open Folder…**. To switch between workspaces, use **File → Open Workspace**.

## The free trial

Jetfold is free to use for 14 days. To keep writing after that, unlock it with a one-time purchase. There's no subscription.

When the trial ends, you can still open, read, search, and export all your notes. Your notes are ordinary files, so you keep them whether or not you buy.

If you bought Jetfold on another Mac, use **Restore Purchases** in **Settings → General** to unlock it on this one.

## The main window

The main window has three columns: your folders, the notes in the selected folder, and the editor.

![The three columns under the toolbar: the window buttons and the folders button over the sidebar, the folder name with the sort and New Note buttons over the note list, and the Note Info and ⋯ buttons over the editor](screenshots/03-columns.png)

To add or remove toolbar buttons, choose **View → Customize Toolbar…**.

Press **⌘1**, **⌘2**, or **⌘3** to show one, two, or all three columns, and hide the rest when you want to focus.

### Turning off what you don't use

Everything except your folders and notes can be switched off. Turn off sidebar sections under **View → Sidebar** and note list details under **View → Note List**. With everything off, Jetfold shows only your folders, your notes, and the editor.

![Jetfold with everything turned off but the folders: the notes folder and subfolders, a list of plain note titles, and the Garden Redesign note](screenshots/61-minimal.png)

## Creating your first note

Press **⌘N**, type a title, press **Return**, and start writing.

Jetfold saves as you type, so there's no Save button.

## Saving and outside changes

If you edit a note in another app, or a sync service downloads a new version, Jetfold updates the open note automatically.

If a note changes on disk **while you have unsaved edits**, Jetfold won't silently overwrite either version. Instead, a bar appears above the note so you can choose which one to keep.

![The "This note changed on disk." bar above a note, with the Keep Mine and Use Disk Version buttons](screenshots/04-conflict.png)

## Appearance

Jetfold matches the Light or Dark appearance set on your Mac. Pick another theme in **Settings → General**. See [Settings and Themes](settings.md#appearance-and-theme).
